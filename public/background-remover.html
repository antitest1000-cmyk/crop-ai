Best Practices for Clean Transparent PNG Cutouts

To ensure optimal edge precision and flawless alpha feathering when removing backgrounds, keep these practical tips in mind:

  • Ensure visible edge contrast: High contrast between your subject and the backdrop yields the most accurate neural edge detection.
  • Use descriptive hints for complex scenes: If the subject shares colors with surrounding objects, add a brief note like "the speaker on the wooden desk".
  • Leverage the Adjust panel: If fine flyaway hair or drop shadows need adjustment, provide a quick refinement prompt without re-uploading.
  • Download as transparent PNG: For compositing over new backgrounds in Figma, Canva, or Photoshop, always save as PNG to retain the alpha channel.
