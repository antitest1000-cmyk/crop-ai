Frequently Asked Questions About Text-Based Image Cropping

How accurately can AI crop an image by text?

CropAI leverages frontier multimodal vision models capable of understanding spatial relationships, relative colors, overlapping subjects, and contextual phrasing. When you enter a prompt like 'the vintage leather chair next to the floor lamp', the AI identifies the exact bounding coordinates with pixel precision, ensuring clean boundaries without clipping edges.

What should I do if my photo contains multiple similar items?

You can provide directional cues in your text prompt, such as 'the red sneaker on the left', 'the person wearing sunglasses', or 'the topmost logo'. If you want every instance, simply prompt in plural (e.g., 'all yellow balloons') and CropAI will detect each item and let you batch-download them.

Can I remove the background while cropping by text?

Yes. Before clicking Process, toggle the 'Remove Background' option. CropAI will locate your specified item via text and execute alpha-matte segmentation to output a transparent PNG of the isolated subject.
