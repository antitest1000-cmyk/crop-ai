Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

🔒 Short version: You upload an image, we process it with AI, return the result, and automatically delete everything from our server within 1 hour. We do not sell your data, we do not train AI models on your images, and we do not require an account.

1. What We Collect

When you use CropAI, we collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Uploaded images: Stored temporarily on our server to process your request. Automatically deleted after 1 hour.
  • Text prompts: The descriptions you type (e.g., "extract the red car"). Sent to the AI provider to analyze your image. Not stored permanently on our servers.
  • Server logs: Standard web server logs including IP address, request timestamps, and HTTP status codes. Retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging.

2. Third-Party AI Processing

Important disclosure: To analyze your images, CropAI sends your image and your text prompt to OpenRouter, a third-party AI routing service, which in turn forwards the request to the underlying vision model (such as Google Gemini, Claude, or GPT-4o).

This means:

  • Your image and prompt are transmitted to OpenRouter's servers and the model provider's servers.
  • We use the free Gemini tier by default. Free-tier models may log or use requests per the model provider's own policies. We strongly recommend reviewing OpenRouter's Privacy Policy and Google's AI data usage terms if this concerns you.
  • If you need guaranteed privacy, consider upgrading to a paid model tier via your own API key.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process your image and return a cropped or extracted result.
  • To monitor server health and debug errors.
  • We do not use your images to train our own AI models.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.

4. Data Retention

  • Uploaded images: Deleted automatically after 1 hour.
  • Result images: Deleted automatically after 1 hour.
  • Server logs: Retained up to 30 days, then purged.
  • No account data: We don't require accounts, so no profile data is stored.

5. Cookies and Analytics

CropAI currently does not use tracking cookies or analytics services. If this changes, we will update this policy and, where required by law, obtain your consent first.

6. Children's Privacy

CropAI is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or 16 in EU countries). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of data sales (we don't sell data, but you may still exercise this right).

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@cropai.app. Since we store almost no persistent data and auto-delete images, most requests will be fulfilled immediately.

8. Security

  • API keys are stored server-side and never exposed to your browser.
  • Uploaded images are stored with random UUID filenames, never publicly guessable.
  • The /uploads and /api paths are not publicly indexable.
  • HTTPS is enforced in production.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of CropAI after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@cropai.app.

Note: This privacy policy was written in good faith but has not been reviewed by a lawyer. For GDPR/CCPA compliance, consider having a qualified legal professional review it.
