Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is CropAI free to use?

Yes. CropAI is completely free and requires no account or credit card. Just upload an image and start cropping.

What happens to my uploaded images?

Your image is sent to our AI provider (OpenRouter / Google Gemini) to detect the object you described. The file on our server is automatically deleted within 1 hour. We do not store, sell, or use your images for any other purpose. Read our Privacy Policy.

How do I crop an object from a photo with AI?

Open the CropAI tool, upload your image, and type a description of what you want to crop — for example, "the dog on the right" or "the logo in the top corner". CropAI's AI finds the object and returns a tight crop in seconds.

Can CropAI remove the background from an image?

Yes. Before submitting your prompt, check the "Remove Background (Transparent PNG)" option. CropAI will isolate the subject and return a transparent PNG, perfect for placing on any background.

What image formats does CropAI support?

CropAI accepts PNG, JPG (JPEG), and WEBP images up to 10 MB in size. Results can be downloaded as PNG or JPG.

What AI model powers CropAI?

CropAI uses state-of-the-art vision language models — Google Gemini by default — accessed via the OpenRouter API. The AI analyzes your image and returns precise object coordinates, which are then used to perform the crop.

What if the AI finds multiple matching objects?

If your prompt is ambiguous, CropAI will show you all matching objects and let you select the ones you want. You can then extract them individually or all at once as a ZIP file.
