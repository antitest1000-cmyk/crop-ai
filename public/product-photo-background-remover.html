Frequently Asked Questions for E-Commerce Sellers

Does CropAI meet Amazon's main image requirement?

Yes. Amazon requires main listing images to display products on a pure, seamless white background (RGB 255, 255, 255) with no distracting props. CropAI extracts your product into a clean transparent PNG, allowing you to easily place it on pure white.

Can I remove backgrounds from multiple product shots in a batch?

Yes. If your photo contains multiple products or packaging variants, CropAI detects each item, lets you select which to extract, and provides an option to download all cutouts together as a ZIP.

Will removing the background lower my product image resolution?

No. CropAI retains native pixel dimensions without aggressive compression so that your images comfortably meet marketplace minimums (such as Amazon's 1000px zoom requirement).
